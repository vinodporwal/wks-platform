# JMD Plant Configuration & Business Context

## 1. Basic Information
- **Overview**: Jamnagar Manufacturing Division (JMD) comprises of DTA-Refinery, SEZ-Refinery, C2 Complex, Gasifier Complex (DTA & SEZ) and associated Offsite/Utilities facilities.
- **Power/Steam Source**: JMD Power/steam requirement is met from Captive Power Plants at DTA / SEZ / C2 / PCG CPPs with equipment like gas turbines, HRSGs, auxiliary boilers, and steam turbines to meet the energy needs of the refinery.
- **Scale**: Jamnagar Refinery requires more than 1 GW of power and >3000 TPH of steam. It is also connected to external grid to source Grid power.
- **Assets**: Across all CPPs, there are more than 30 power generation units and equivalent quantity of steam generation units. Additional steam needs are met via supplementary firing in HRSGs and auxiliary boilers.

## 2. Interconnectivity of Networks
- **Electrical**: The electrical network operates at a 220-kV level, interconnecting CPPs at DTA, SEZ, C2, and PCG.
- **Steam**: Steam networks are interlinked at various pressure levels between DTA, SEZ, and PCG, while the **C2 steam network operates in Islanding mode** (i.e. no interconnectivity with DTA/SEZ/PCG).

## 3. Fuel and KPI
- **Fuel Flexibility**: Fuel flexibility for power & steam generation comprises of Syngas as primary fuel and NG as incremental fuel after saturating RFG consumption. Liquid fuel is used based on economics.
- **KPI**: Heat rates of all equipment are closely monitored as key performance indicators (KPIs) to drive efficiency.

## 4. Asset Performance Parameters
### 4.1. Gas Turbines
- **FR6**: Operated at 80-100% of capacity with heat rates of 2800-3000 Kcal/kWh.
- **FR9**: Operated at 80-100% of capacity with heat rates of 2600-2800 Kcal/kWh.

### 4.2. HRSGs
- **FR6**: Operated at 80-95% of capacity with heat rates of 550-600 Mcal/MT.
- **FR9**: Operated at 80-95% of capacity with heat rates of 570-600 Mcal/MT.

### 4.3. Auxiliary Boilers
- **DTA**: Operated 40-70% of capacity with heat rates of 750-800 Mcal/MT to maintain steam margin.
- **SEZ, C2**: Operated at turndown load with heat rates of 600-700 Mcal/MT to maintain steam margin.

## 5. Network Flow Context
- **Steam Flow**: SEZ CPP, DTA CPP, SEZ PCG, and DTA PCG are interconnected with HP, HHP, and LLP lines. C2 CPP operates isolated, feeding IIR with HHP and LP steam.
- **Power Flow**: DTA CPP acts as a central distribution hub with 220kV connections to DTA MRS, SEZ CPP, and C2 CPP MRS. SEZ CPP distributes further to SEZ MRS and PX 4. Local 33kV lines feed specific complex loads (e.g. Gasification Complexes).
